## Overall Validation Assessment

### Scoring Rubric

**Each dimension scored as:**
- ✅ **STRONG (3 points):** All or most success criteria met
- ⚠️ **MODERATE (2 points):** Some criteria met, refinement needed
- ❌ **WEAK (1 point):** Few criteria met, significant issues

**Total Score:** Sum of 7 dimensions (max 21 points)

### Interpretation

| Total Score | Assessment | Recommendation |
|-------------|------------|----------------|
| **18-21 points** | ✅ **STRONG Product-Market Fit** | Pursue pilot with real data, seek institutional partnerships |
| **14-17 points** | ⚠️ **MODERATE Potential** | Refine product based on feedback, conduct additional validation |
| **10-13 points** | ⚠️ **WEAK Validation** | Major refinements needed, reconsider approach or target users |
| **<10 points** | ❌ **POOR Fit** | Reconsider product positioning, pivot, or terminate project |

---

## Decision Matrix

### GO: Proceed to Pilot (Score ≥14 AND specific conditions met)

**Required Conditions:**
1. ✅ Problem Validation: STRONG or MODERATE
2. ✅ Solution Validation: STRONG or MODERATE
3. ✅ Institutional Fit: STRONG or MODERATE
4. ✅ At least 2 potential champions identified
5. ✅ Data access timeline ≤6 months
6. ✅ No insurmountable technical or organizational barriers

**Next Steps:**
- Refine dashboard based on feedback
- Develop pilot scope and timeline
- Initiate data access negotiations
- Engage institutional champions
- Prepare pilot success criteria

---

### REFINE: Iterate on Product (Score 10-13 OR missing 1-2 GO conditions)

**Required Actions:**
1. Address critical feature gaps identified in interviews
2. Recalibrate thresholds based on user feedback
3. Improve UX/clarity if comprehension is weak
4. Conduct additional demonstrations with refined version
5. Re-assess validation criteria after refinements

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks for refinement + re-validation

---

### RECONSIDER: Pivot or Terminate (Score <10 OR major issues)

**Indicators for Reconsideration:**
- Users consistently state tool duplicates existing systems
- No institutional champions willing to support
- Data access insurmountable (legal, privacy, organizational barriers)
- Problem validation fails (users don't struggle with bottleneck identification)
- Solution validation fails (dashboard not useful even after refinements)

**Options:**
1. **Pivot:** Target different user group or use case
2. **Reframe:** Adjust value proposition or positioning
3. **Terminate:** Acknowledge poor product-market fit, learn from validation

## Validation Data Collection

### Quantitative Data Sources

**Post-Demonstration Survey (Administered to 8-10 participants):**
- Usefulness: 1-5 scale
- Clarity: 1-5 scale
- Credibility: 1-5 scale
- Actionability: 1-5 scale
- Pilot Interest: Yes / Maybe / No
- Information Gap: Addresses gap / Duplicates tools

**Demonstration Observations (8-10 sessions):**
- Bottleneck identification accuracy (correct/incorrect)
- Scenario distinction (LOW vs MODERATE vs HIGH)
- Purpose articulation (correct/incorrect)
- Confusion points (documented by facilitator)

**Interview Responses (6-8 interviews):**
- Problem confirmation (yes/no + severity rating)
- Existing tool inventory (list of tools)
- Missing features (list of requests)
- Adoption barriers (list of barriers)
- Pilot interest and conditions

### Qualitative Data Sources

- Verbatim quotes from interviews (key insights, concerns, praise)
- Use cases described by participants
- Feature requests and design suggestions
- Institutional context (data governance, org structure, priorities)
- Competitive landscape (what tools exist, what's missing)

---

## Success Indicators (Qualitative)

**Strong Positive Signals:**
- ✅ Participants immediately understand dashboard purpose
- ✅ Participants describe specific decisions they would make based on insights
- ✅ Participants ask detailed questions about features or data
- ✅ Participants spontaneously compare favorably to existing tools
- ✅ Participants offer to introduce other stakeholders
- ✅ Participants express enthusiasm or excitement about concept

**Moderate Positive Signals:**
- ⚠️ Participants understand but uncertain about usefulness until they see real data
- ⚠️ Participants identify value but also significant missing features
- ⚠️ Participants interested but concerned about institutional barriers
- ⚠️ Participants find some indicators useful, others irrelevant

**Negative Signals:**
- ❌ Participants struggle to understand metrics or purpose
- ❌ Participants cannot identify actionable insights from scenarios
- ❌ Participants state "we already have this"
- ❌ Participants express skepticism about value proposition
- ❌ Participants identify insurmountable barriers (data, org, resources)
- ❌ Participants disengage or appear uninterested

## Assumptions and Limitations

**Assumptions:**
1. 8-10 demonstrations and 6-8 interviews provide sufficient signal for decision-making
2. Participants can assess usefulness based on synthetic-data demonstration
3. Quantitative thresholds (≥60%, ≥50%) are appropriate for early-stage validation
4. Interview participants represent broader target user population

**Limitations:**
1. Small sample size (not statistically significant)
2. Self-reported assessments (not behavioral observation)
3. Synthetic data limits realism of demonstration
4. Selection bias (participants who agree to participate may be more interested)
5. Single-point-in-time assessment (not longitudinal usage data)

**Mitigations:**
- Triangulate quantitative and qualitative data
- Seek diverse participant perspectives (roles, regions, experience levels)
- Explicitly acknowledge limitations in decision rationale
- Plan for pilot phase to validate findings with real usage
