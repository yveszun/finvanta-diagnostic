BOTTLENECK LOGIC CLARIFICATION
================================================================================

Primary Bottleneck = Stage with LOWEST CONVERSION RATE

NOT the largest absolute facility drop.

Example:
  - Accreditation: 70% conversion (300 facilities lost) ← BOTTLENECK
  - Claims:        86% conversion (100 facilities lost)
  - Payment:       92% conversion (50 facilities lost)

The interface displays BOTH metrics:
  ✓ Conversion Rate (%) ← Used to identify bottleneck
  ✓ Facilities Lost     ← Shows impact

This is accurate and clearly labeled in the UI.
